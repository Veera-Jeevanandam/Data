General Health Guidelines:
- Adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic activity per week.
- Muscle-strengthening activities should be done on 2 or more days a week.
- A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats.
- Limit added sugars, saturated fats, and sodium.

Diabetes Management:
- Regular physical activity helps control blood glucose levels.
- Monitor carbohydrate intake.
- Aim for a healthy weight.

Hypertension Management:
- Reduce sodium intake.
- Increase potassium intake through foods like bananas and spinach.
- Limit alcohol consumption.
- Regular aerobic exercise can lower blood pressure.

Cholesterol Management:
- Reduce saturated and trans fats.
- Eat foods rich in omega-3 fatty acids.
- Increase soluble fiber intake.

Obesity Management:
- Create a caloric deficit for weight loss.
- Focus on nutrient-dense foods.
- Incorporate both aerobic and resistance training.
